ABOUT COMMUNITY

The Harbour is slated to be a 2-tower condo project with 66 floors and 425 units located on Biscayne Boulevard in Aventura in Miami-Dade County. Enjoy the best of nature and the outdoors in a place unlike any other. A lifestyle unmatched anywhere else in the world. Everything you need. Everything your heart desires.It’s all right here. The Harbour makes the entire Miami experience yours like never before.

Aqua Club
Fulfill your every desire in the bright turquoise waters of the beach and the Aqua Club. Sophisticated amenities are plentiful here – with activities coordinators available to help you choose from a wide variety of water activities including; boating, paddle boards, kayaks, windsurfing, sailing, scuba diving and fishing. Enjoy a delightful meal at our exclusive waterfront restaurant or relax on the beach sipping a cocktail in your own private cabana.

The Pool
Like your own private-island retreat, The Harbour immerses every moment in the natural beauty of the water. Sundecks and lounges seem to float on the surface. Palms gently sway in the breeze. And the pool’s infinity edge dissolves all barriers between you and the bay.

Outdoor Activities and Sports
No matter what your game, you’ll not only find a place here to play, you’ll even find the equipment you need. Our extensive sports facilities, courts and multi-purpose field are perfect for sports lovers. Racquets, balls, as well as bicycles for exploring, are readily available on request.

The exercise enthusiasts do not need to feel left out – come experience the latest exercise trend at our state of the art Vita Course with various outdoor exercise stations thoughtfully designed to maximize your workout. A perfectly planned course, a full route around the trail provides a complete cardiovascular and muscle workout.

Aromatherapy Trail
Rejuvenate the mind. Invigorate the body. And awaken your senses during a peaceful walk or run through the Aromatherapy Trail. Discover carefully chosen plants whose restorative powers of aromatherapy can help heal the soul and body.

The Lush Spa
Inspired by nature and balance, our indoor/outdoor Lush Spa is your private sanctuary of bliss and wellness. Enjoy a massage with the tranquil sounds of water and chirping birds all around you. Feel the warmth of rich wood decks and soft lounge areas bidding you to relax and unwind. Or discover the restorative essential oil treatments, aromas and beautiful vistas of the outdoors.

Interiors and Appliances
Spacious bathrooms with deep soaker tubs offer respite and serenity. Elegant and stylish, with the modern conveniences you expect, your home at The Harbour falls nothing short of inspired.
