The Harbour Miami Building

The Harbour Condominium is a 32-story high-rise residential building along Maule Lake at 16385 Biscayne Blvd, North Miami Beach, FL 33160.

This private gated community of twin waterfront residential buildings was developed in 2018 by 13th Floor Investment and Key International in collaboration with Corwil Architectural firm.

The Towers sits on five acres of land on Maule Lake next to Marina Palms South Tower, surrounded by calm coastal waters and lush botanical gardens of palm trees, cycads, flowering trees, and vines.

Quick Facts About Harbour Miami

  • Each tower has 32 stories
  • 425 luxury residences
  • Private elevators
  • European kitchen cabinetry
  • Nine feet of ceilings
  • Units from one bedroom plus a Den to three bedrooms plus a Den
  • Floor plans from 1,188 – 2,010 SqFt.

In addition, The Harbor building in North Miami Beach sits between Miami and Ft Lauderdale Airports. It is only a few miles from The Bal Harbour Shops in the Bal Harbour Villages and The Turnberry Isles Gulf Course in Aventura.
