The Harbour Tops Off with 32-Story Twin Towers

August 18, 2017 / Press Release, South Florida

Luxury residential project in North Miami Beach on track for 2018 delivery

MIAMI – August, 2017 – Developers 13th Floor Investments and Key International, in conjunction with Moss, announce that The Harbour, a twin-tower, 425-unit luxury waterfront condominium located in North Miami Beach, Fla., has officially topped off construction at 32 stories for both towers. The $300 million project broke ground in February 2016 and remains on track with its scheduled delivery in late 2018.

With 90 percent of its units already sold and under contract, The Harbour marks the third venture between Moss, 13th Floor Investments and Key International. Together, they are in the final stages of delivering 1010 Brickell, a 387-unit luxury building in the heart of Miami’s Financial District, and 400 Sunny Isles, a sold-out waterfront condo comprised of 230 high-end residential units and a dry boat storage facility.

“The Harbour is yet another testament to the success that can be achieved when working with a strong development partner and best-in-class general contractor,” said 13th Floor Investments Chief Operating Officer, Rey Melendi. “Our collective development expertise and global marketing reach has helped unlock an international buyer pool seeking luxury waterfront living and resort-style amenities.”

Plans for The Harbour originally called for two 25-story towers housing 330 units however, due to overwhelming demand, the developers expanded the project’s scope by nearly 30 percent. The condo units’ high-end offerings will feature open-concept floorplans, private elevators, expansive private balconies and direct water views of Biscayne Bay. Residences include 1-, 2- and 3-bedroom units as well as penthouses and two-story villas, ranging from 1,032 to 2,442 square feet with prices starting from the $500,000’s.

“Miami’s waterfront is one of its most iconic assets, and The Harbour is designed to create a private oasis experience in a highly sought-after neighborhood,” said Key International President, Inigo Ardid. “As buildable land becomes increasingly scarce, this is one of the few new projects to provide residents with unobstructed, direct water views.”

The Harbour spans four-and-a-half acres and offers a wide range of luxury amenities, including a full-service private beach club with a panoramic pool. It also features an Aromatherapy Trail, offering owners a unique guided path around the property through a series of curated rejuvenating vegetation.

“This is the third time we have had the pleasure of working with Key International and 13th Floor Investments,” said Moss & Associates Executive Vice President, Scott Desharnais. “We enjoy working with clients with whom we have a long-standing relationship because there is an ease of communication and a shared understanding of the extraordinary goal being accomplished.”

The Harbour’s exclusive Lush Spa will also offer a full menu of treatment and therapies both indoors and outdoors, as well as hydrotherapy plunge pools, steam and sauna rooms amid lavish botanical gardens and exquisite landscaping. Additional resident amenities include an Exercise Vita Course, a state-of-the-art fitness center, concierge, outdoor food and drink service, dog park, barbecue facilities, a soccer field, tennis and basketball courts, children’s wet and dry playgrounds, arcade and more.

For additional information, please visit theharbourmiami.com or call Key International Sales, The Harbour’s luxury home specialists, at 305-377-1025.

About 13th Floor Investments
13th Floor Investments is a Miami, Florida based investor, owner, developer, and manager of real estate properties. Adhering to a strict value-investment thesis, the firm seeks to generate superior, risk-adjusted returns for its investors while actively mitigating risk. Through its family of real estate fund vehicles, the firm acquires and develops real estate across all asset classes across the state of Florida, both directly and through strategic partnerships with like-minded firms. 13th Floor has invested over $400MM of equity in 43 transactions since 2008, representing a total estimated project value of over $1.5 Billion. Current assets within the company’s portfolio include: luxury condominium developments (400 Sunny Isles and 1010 Brickell); multifamily developments (Peary Court in Key West and Motion in Dadeland); single family home communities (Central Parc and Manor Parc in Tamarac); and commercial property (Highland Park Center in Miami and 3650 Design Plaza in Miami’s Design District). Learn more at www.13fi.com.

About Key International
Key International is a real estate investment and development company with offices in Miami, Florida and Madrid, Spain. The company has developed over 10 Million square feet of property and has done over $4 Billion of diversified real estate transactions. It focuses primarily on high end commercial and luxury residential properties including condominiums, hotels and resorts, office buildings, residential rentals, and retail properties. Some projects include Eden Roc Resort Miami Beach, Marriott Stanton Hotel South Beach, 400 Sunny Isles, 1010 Brickell, and the master development of Riverfront Community (a 13.5-acre gated community in Downtown Miami that will be home to over 2,000 residential units). Key International also has a best-in-class condominium sales platform with experienced leadership having sold over $5 Billion of real estate to domestic and a broad range of international clients. It is an experienced operator, currently managing a real estate portfolio worth over $2 Billion. Please see key-international.com for more information on Key International and its portfolio.

About Moss
Moss (www.mosscm.com) is a national privately held construction firm providing innovative solutions resulting in award-winning projects. With nine regional offices across the United States, Moss focuses on construction management at-risk, design-build, and public-private partnerships. The company’s diverse portfolio encompasses a wide range of sectors, including luxury high-rise residential, landmark mixed-use developments, hospitality, primary and higher education, justice and solar energy. Moss prides itself on a strong entrepreneurial culture that honors safety, quality, client engagement and employee development. Its employees consistently rank Moss as one of the best places to work.
