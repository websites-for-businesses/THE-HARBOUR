Welcome

Tucked away along the coastal waters of North Miami Beach, The Harbour is a private, gated community offering twin 32-story towers with 425 luxury residences. This development features private elevators and open-concept floor plans with modern interiors, surrounded by calm coastal waters and lush botanical gardens. The Harbour provides a prime location just minutes from Aventura Mall, Bal Harbour Shops, and Sunny Isles Beaches. The community boasts an infinity pool and a suite of luxury amenities, making it a perfect private oasis.

Location

Located in North Miami Beach, The Harbour Condo offers a hidden sanctuary surrounded by pristine coastal waters and lush botanical gardens. This luxury condo community is set on five acres along Sunny Isles Bay, just minutes from world-class shopping at Aventura Mall and Bal Harbour Shops, chic eateries, Florida state parks, and the beautiful beaches of Sunny Isles and Bal Harbour. The Harbour is your private oasis of perfection.

Modern Living

The Harbour offers an array of modern residences and penthouses, each featuring semi-private elevators and open-concept layouts. These elegant living spaces include sophisticated interiors, European-style kitchens, contemporary bathrooms, and expansive balconies accessible from living rooms and master bedrooms. Details such as smooth-finished ceilings ranging from 9′ to 12′, spacious walk-in wardrobes in master bedrooms, and floor-to-ceiling sliding-glass doors enhance the luxury. Each unit offers breathtaking views of the Intracoastal, the ocean, and the city.

Outdoor Amenities

Outdoor amenities at The Harbour feature an infinity-edge pool overlooking the water, an expansive sundeck, a peaceful serenity trail, a fenced dog park, children's play areas, a large BBQ space, sports facilities for tennis, soccer, and basketball, a restaurant, and a well-equipped Aqua Club that offers a variety of water sports activities.

Indoor Amenities

Indoor amenities at The Harbour include a state-of-the-art fitness center. The center also features top-brand weight training machines and an extensive free-weight collection. An open studio accommodates yoga, Pilates, and aerobics, complemented by an additional outdoor yoga area. Certified personal trainers and instructor-led classes are available to enhance your fitness experience.
