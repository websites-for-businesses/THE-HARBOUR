About Us

Welcome to The Harbour Condo: Your Private Retreat in North Miami Beach

Tucked away along the coastal waters of North Miami Beach, The Harbour is a private, gated community offering twin 32-story towers with 425 luxury residences. This development features private elevators and open-concept floor plans with modern interiors, surrounded by calm coastal waters and lush botanical gardens. The Harbour provides a prime location just minutes from Aventura Mall, Bal Harbour Shops, and Sunny Isles Beaches. The community boasts an infinity pool and a suite of luxury amenities, making it a perfect private oasis.

The Harbour Condo Features

The Harbour Condo offers the very best in personalized and six-star services. Virtually every detail is customized and features the best in architectural solution, layout options, high-end finishes and epic views.

  • 425 luxury residences in two 32 story towers with private elevators
  • Stunning architectural design by Corwil Architects
  • Multi-story lobby with panoramic pool and bay views
  • Elegant interiors by The Design Agency
  • Planned private marina and boat slips with bay and ocean access
  • Full-service Waterfront Club with daybeds and lounges
  • Adult relaxation sundeck
  • Infinity-edge pool with bay views
  • Indoor/outdoor restaurant and bar
  • Aqua Activities Club with personal coordinators offering various amenities
  • Serenity Trail with Vita Course fitness stations
  • Kids areas: Arcade Room, Water Play Zone, Playground
  • Sports facilities: tennis, racquetball, soccer, basketball
  • Outdoor BBQ and gathering area
  • Fenced dog park
  • Exclusive concierge, dry-cleaning, and laundry services
  • Smart home technology installed
  • One assigned covered parking space per residence
  • Secure covered parking with 24-hour surveillance and valet service
  • 24-hour on-site security personnel and video monitoring
