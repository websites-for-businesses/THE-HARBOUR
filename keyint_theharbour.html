The Harbour, North Miami Beach

Residential

The Harbour

North Miami Beach

  • 425-unit luxury waterfront condominium
  • Units feature open floor plans, private elevators, and expansive balconies.
  • Infinity-edge pool, full service Lush Spa, children’s play zone, social lounge, and sporting facilities
  • 5-acre property overlooking Biscayne Bay Aquatic Preserve.

The Harbour is a two tower 425-unit luxury waterfront condominium. The Harbour offers the ideal Miami experience – a combination of waterfront views, sleek design, unique amenities, curated natural surroundings, and convenient access to shopping and nightlife.

This almost five-acre project is located at the crossroads of Aventura, Sunny Isles Beach, and North Miami Beach. Units feature open-concept floor plans, private elevators, expansive balconies, striking city skylines, and stunning, direct water views of the Biscayne Bay Aquatic Preserve.

The Harbour also features a plethora of unique amenities including water activities at the Aqua Club, treatment rooms, social lounge with billiards, games, and large flat screen TVs, a children’s play zone, a dog park, a fully equipped gym, numerous sporting facilities like tennis, soccer, basketball, and racquetball.

The building was delivered at the end of Q4 2018. The project was designed by Corweil Architects.

Location

16385 Biscayne Blvd, North Miami Beach, FL 33160
