The Property

Two towers.
One uninterrupted shoreline.

Rising thirty-two stories above Biscayne Bay, The Harbour's North and South towers were developed by 13th Floor Investments as a waterfront residential community at 16385 Biscayne Boulevard — where the bay, a protected mangrove shoreline, and the Miami skyline meet.

Residences range from open one-bedroom-plus-den layouts to expansive penthouses and two-story waterfront villas, each designed with private terraces, floor-to-ceiling glass, and bay or marina views.
